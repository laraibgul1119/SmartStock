SmartStock

A simple web-based inventory and sales management system for small shops and retail businesses. Manage products, record purchases and sales, monitor stock levels and get low-stock alerts — all in one centralized platform.

Why SmartStock?

  • Automated stock updates on every purchase and sale
  • Accurate sales, purchase and inventory records
  • Low-stock and out-of-stock notifications
  • Dashboard and reports for business performance

Features

Everything a small shop needs to run smoothly.

Product Management

Add product categories, products and supplier details in one place.

Sales Recording

Record customer sales, generate receipts and decrease stock automatically.

Purchase Records

Record purchases from suppliers and increase available stock instantly.

Stock Alerts

Automatically identifies low-stock and out-of-stock products.

Reports

User Management

Receipts

How It Works

The main flow of the system.

1
Login

The user logs into the system using their registered account.

2
Setup

The shop owner adds product categories, products and supplier details.

3
Purchases

Purchase is recorded and available stock is automatically increased.

4
Sales

The cashier records the sale and stock is automatically decreased.

5
Monitoring

The system identifies low-stock and out-of-stock products.

6
Reports

View sales history, stock information and business performance.
